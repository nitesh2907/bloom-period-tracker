Knows when it's time to see a doctor

Bloom shows a clear "see a doctor" alert for severe pain, very heavy flow, clots, dizziness, periods longer than 7 days, or cycles shorter than 21 or longer than 35 days. Bloom gives general tips and is not a replacement for medical advice.

Your data stays on your phone

No account, no cloud, no ads tracking you. Your dates, symptoms and doctor contacts are saved only on your device. Delete the app and it's all gone. Read the privacy policy.

Questions

Good to know

Is Bloom really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no subscription and no sign-up.

Does it work on both Android and iPhone?

Yes. Bloom is built from one codebase for Android and iOS, so both get the same features.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Bloom has no servers that store your period data, and nothing is uploaded.

Does it need the internet?

No. Tracking, reminders and pad alerts all work offline. You only need the internet to order pads or check for app updates.

Can Bloom diagnose a health problem?

No. Bloom gives general food and self-care tips and tells you when your symptoms are worth showing a doctor. Always follow your doctor's advice.
