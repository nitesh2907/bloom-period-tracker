Bloom

Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Bloom is a period tracker app for Android and iOS. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: everything you enter stays on your phone.

What Bloom stores

  • Period start and end dates you log
  • Daily flow, pain level and symptoms
  • Your pad stock count
  • Doctor names and phone numbers you save

All of this is saved only in the app's local storage on your device. Bloom has no account system and no server that receives this data.

What Bloom does not do

  • It does not upload, sell or share your health data
  • It does not use advertising or analytics trackers
  • It does not ask for your name, email or location

Notifications

Bloom asks for permission to send notifications so it can remind you before your period and warn you when pad stock is low. These reminders are scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off any time in your phone's settings.

Links to other apps

When you tap Blinkit, Swiggy Instamart, Zepto or BigBasket, Bloom opens that app or website with a search for pads. When you tap Call or WhatsApp, it opens your phone's dialer or WhatsApp. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Update check

Bloom may download a small public file to check whether a newer version is available. This request does not include any of your personal data.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Bloom deletes all of your data from your phone. Because nothing is stored elsewhere, there is nothing left for us to delete.

Health information

Tips in Bloom are general information and are not medical advice. If you are worried about your health, please talk to a doctor.

Contact
